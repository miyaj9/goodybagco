Instacart vs DasherMart

About

I'm Miya Jordan, a graduate student in Human-Computer Interaction. My work sits at the intersection of design, ethics, and technology, particularly around how AI systems affect people who are already marginalized.

Before focusing on design, I studied research methods and ethics, which means I approach design problems from the user's reality first, not the interface. I'm currently building out my Figma and prototyping skills alongside my research practice.

I'm interested in roles in product design, product management, and AI-forward product teams — anywhere thoughtful strategy meets human-centered execution.
